About Mangaluru Bus Timings

Live bus arrival times for your daily commute.

What this site does

Mangaluru Bus Timings shows the scheduled arrival time of the next buses at every stop. Search for your stop to see live "time to next bus", or plan a trip by choosing a From and To stop to see which buses connect them and when they leave.

How it works

  • Timings are entered and maintained by the site administrators.
  • The public site reads the schedule directly — there is no live GPS tracking, so times are scheduled estimates and may vary with traffic.
  • QR codes posted at stops link straight to that stop's page.

Good to know

  • Times are shown in Indian Standard Time (IST).
  • Holiday and special schedules are handled automatically when published by the admin.
  • Always confirm on the bus or with the conductor before boarding.
