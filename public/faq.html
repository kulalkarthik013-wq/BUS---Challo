Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check when my bus arrives?

Search for your bus stop on the home page, or scan the QR code at the stop. You'll see every scheduled arrival in order, with the bus name, registration number, route and destination.

Are the times real-time?

No. Times shown are from the published schedule, not live GPS tracking. Traffic and other delays can make buses arrive late. We always recommend leaving a few minutes early.

How do I plan a journey from one stop to another?

Use the From → To tab on the home page. Pick your starting and destination stops from the suggestions and tap Find buses. You'll see all direct services with departure and arrival times.

What does the QR code at my stop do?

Scanning it opens this site's page for that stop, showing upcoming arrivals. The code links to a permanent URL, so printed codes keep working even if the stop is renamed.

Why is a bus missing from the list?

Possible reasons: the bus already departed (it drops off the "next buses" list), it isn't scheduled at that stop, or the schedule hasn't been updated yet. If a bus consistently doesn't appear, let the operator know via the Contact page.

What do "Today" and "Tomorrow" show on a stop page?

Today shows the next hour of arrivals by default. Tap the arrow to expand and see the next 30 minutes and all later buses today. Tomorrow shows the full schedule for that stop the following day.

The site says it's under maintenance. What does that mean?

The operator has temporarily paused public access — usually for schedule updates or fixes. The page shows a countdown to when service resumes. Administrators can still use the site normally.

Is my data safe?

We collect almost nothing — just anonymous usage counts to improve the service. See our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for details.

How do I report a wrong time or missing bus?

Use the Contact page and include the stop name, bus name/registration, and the scheduled time that looks wrong.

Can I use this for a school, office or fleet?

Yes — the schedule data is maintained by the site operator. For bulk QR printing or custom setups, contact us and we'll help.
