Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. What we collect

Mangaluru Bus Timings is designed to collect as little data as possible. We collect:

  • Usage analytics — anonymous counts of page views, QR-code scans and from→to searches, used only to show the admin dashboard which stops are popular.
  • Cookies & local storage — a single flag remembering your cookie-consent choice, plus standard browser storage for the site to function. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

We do not require accounts, collect names, emails, phone numbers, or precise location from visitors.

2. How we use data

  • Show live bus arrival times and plan journeys.
  • Help operators understand which stops and routes are used most, so schedules can be improved.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Anonymous analytics are stored in our database provider (Supabase) and are visible only to site administrators. QR codes encode only a public stop link — they contain no personal information.

4. Data retention

Analytics events are retained indefinitely in aggregated form. You can ask us to delete analytics data associated with a stop at any time via the Contact page.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR/India's DPDP Act where applicable), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we collect virtually no personal data, most requests can be handled by clearing your browser storage.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by row-level security policies. Admin access requires authenticated credentials.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the Contact page.
