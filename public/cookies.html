Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

What we use

Bus Timings uses a minimal set of cookies and browser storage. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or any technology that follows you across other websites.

Cookies & storage we set

  • Consent flag (localStorage, bt-consent) — remembers whether you accepted or rejected the cookie banner so we don't ask on every visit. Expires only when you clear your browser data.
  • Session preferences (sessionStorage) — remembers dismissed announcements and seen popups for the current browser session only.
  • Authentication session — if you sign in to the admin panel, Supabase stores a secure session token so you stay signed in. This never applies to public visitors.

What we don't use

  • No third-party advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking pixels.
  • No sale of browsing data to data brokers.

Your choices

You can accept or reject non-essential storage via the banner shown on your first visit. Rejecting does not break the site — timings, search and QR features all work without it. You can also clear cookies and site data at any time through your browser settings.

Changes

If we ever introduce additional cookies, this page will be updated and the consent banner will ask again.
